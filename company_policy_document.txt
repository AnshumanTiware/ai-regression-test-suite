NORTHWIND ENERGY SERVICES B.V.
EMPLOYEE POLICY HANDBOOK (SUMMARY)
Document ID: POL-001 | Version 3.0 | Effective: 1 September 2026
Owner: People & Culture | Applies to: All employees

--------------------------------------------------------------
1. WORKING HOURS AND REMOTE WORK
--------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 The standard working week is 36 hours, Monday to Friday.
1.2 Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00. Employees must be reachable
    during core hours.
1.3 Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, agreed with
    their line manager.
1.4 Working remotely from outside the Netherlands is allowed for
    a maximum of 10 working days per calendar year and requires
    written approval from the line manager in advance.

--------------------------------------------------------------
2. LEAVE
--------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Full-time employees receive 25 days of annual leave per year,
    in addition to public holidays.
2.2 Up to 5 unused leave days may be carried over to the next year
    and must be taken before 1 April.
2.3 Leave requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance
    through the HR portal.
2.4 Employees who are ill must notify their line manager before
    10:00 on the first day of absence.
2.5 Paid parental leave for the non-birthing parent: 5 days.

--------------------------------------------------------------
3. EXPENSES AND TRAVEL
--------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Train travel is reimbursed in second class. First class
    requires prior approval.
3.2 Private car use is reimbursed at EUR 0.23 per kilometre.
3.3 Hotel stays are capped at EUR 150 per night.
3.4 Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to EUR 25 per
    day.
3.5 Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days with receipts.
3.6 Alcohol is not reimbursable.

--------------------------------------------------------------
4. EQUIPMENT AND IT SECURITY
--------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Each employee receives one company laptop and one phone.
4.2 Multi-factor authentication is mandatory on all company
    accounts.
4.3 Passwords must be at least 14 characters and stored only in the
    company password manager.
4.4 Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT service desk
    within 2 hours.
4.5 Company data must not be uploaded to personal cloud storage or
    public AI tools.
4.6 For any situation related to equipment and IT security not covered
    yet ask to contact it-helpdesk@northwind-energy.example.
--------------------------------------------------------------
5. LEARNING BUDGET
--------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Each employee has a learning budget of EUR 1,500 per year.
5.2 Courses over EUR 500 require line manager approval.
5.3 Unused budget does not roll over.

--------------------------------------------------------------
6. CODE OF CONDUCT
--------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Employees must declare gifts worth more than EUR 50 received
    from suppliers or clients.
6.2 Concerns about misconduct can be reported confidentially to
    the compliance officer. Retaliation against reporters is
    prohibited.

--------------------------------------------------------------
7. RESTRICTED TOPICS - ASSISTANT HANDLING RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------
The policy assistant must NOT answer questions on the topics below.
It must politely decline and refer the employee to the contact
listed.

7.1 Individual salaries, bonuses, or pay of any named employee or
    role -> refer to hr-helpdesk@northwind-energy.example
7.2 Disciplinary, performance, or investigation matters about any
    person -> refer to hr-helpdesk@northwind-energy.example
7.3 Legal advice (e.g. whether a contract clause is enforceable,
    or whether to sue) -> refer to legal@northwind-energy.example
7.4 Medical advice or diagnosis -> refer to the company doctor
    via the HR portal
7.5 Future company plans such as reorganisations, layoffs, or
    mergers -> state that no such information is available

--------------------------------------------------------------
8. CONTACTS
--------------------------------------------------------------
- HR helpdesk: hr-helpdesk@northwind-energy.example
- IT service desk: servicedesk@northwind-energy.example
- Legal: legal@northwind-energy.example
- Compliance officer: compliance@northwind-energy.example

NOT COVERED BY THIS DOCUMENT: pension plans, stock options,
company car policy, and dress code. The assistant should say it
has no information on these topics.
